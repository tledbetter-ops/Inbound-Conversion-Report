How this is calculated

Sale: result is Application Submitted (including transferred to BAT and double sale). Enrollment appointments scheduled are shown on their own and are not counted as sales. Sales AHT: average time in call on sale calls. Non-Sales AHT: average time in call on every other Opp. Dead air and calls under 60 seconds are left out of both and shown as their own counts. Flags (agents need at least 8 Opps, 4 on a single day): Below goal is conversion under 13.32%. Possible avoidance is below goal with Non-Sales AHT under 75% of the team average. Long AHT is Sales or Non-Sales AHT over 140% of the team average. Dead air is 3 or more dead-air calls. Dead air % is dead-air calls ÷ answered calls; 10% or more shows in red. Under 60s is answered calls shorter than 60 seconds that were not dead air; Under 60s % divides that by answered calls, and 20% or more shows in red. Wrap %, Pause % and Hours logged come from the end-of-day files and cover all time, inbound and outbound: total wrap or total pause ÷ total hours logged in. Wrap above 5% and pause above 20% show in red; L3 and L4 agents (marked *) include Admin Work in pause, so they are not flagged for pause. OPAH is Opps per agent hour and SPAH is sales per agent hour, using inbound sales-queue Opps and sales over total hours logged in. Low hours or high pause/wrap can explain low volume. Flags list every one that applies: Below goal, Possible avoidance, Long AHT, Dead air (3+ calls), High wrap, High pause, High dead air % (10%+) and High under 60s % (20%+).
